## Data quality

- **Artifact-risk mix is heavily skewed high** (41/59 high, 10 low, 8 medium). The universe as pulled was dominated by names where the +20% surprise figure is likely a low-base or one-off artifact rather than a genuine beat. Operational note for any future PEAD design: pre-filter or stratify on artifact risk *before* entry, not after.
- **Revenue-leg coverage is thin:** only 27/59 revenue-confirmed yes, 30 no, 2 unknown. If a future experiment wants to condition on revenue confirmation, the annotation pipeline needs to resolve unknowns pre-entry.
- Avg surprise 425.8% confirms the artifact skew — the tape is full of tiny-denominator beats.
