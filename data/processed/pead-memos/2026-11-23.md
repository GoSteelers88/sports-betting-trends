# Weekly Research Memo — 2026-11-23

## Status

**Experiment No. 3: KILLED by pre-registered rule.**

At n=60 settled (threshold n=40), mean excess return vs SPY = **−4.65%**, t = **−2.26**. The kill rule ("mean excess ≤ 0 at n≥40 kills") is triggered decisively. Realized P&L: **−$1,103.74**. 17 positions remain open; per protocol they are held to their 28-day exits and booked, but no new entries are taken.

The hypothesis — that a naive EPS-beat ≥ +20% long with a 28-day hold generates positive excess return vs SPY under paper-taker execution — is **rejected** at the pre-registered sample size. This is the designed outcome of a kill rule: we spent ~$1.1k of paper P&L and 60 observations to learn the rule doesn't work as specified. That is a successful experiment.

## Observations

- Headline result (n=60, t=−2.26) is statistically meaningful in the rejection direction, not merely noisy-negative.
- All subgroup splits below are **preliminary / forking-path territory** and were not pre-registered. Noted, not acted on:
  - Revenue-confirmed Yes (n=28, −3.08%, t=−1.38) vs No (n=30, −5.35%, t=−1.53): directionally consistent with a revenue-quality story but neither cell is significant and the gap is within noise.
  - Artifact Low (n=11) vs Med/High (n=49): Low cell is too small to interpret; both negative.
  - Surprise bucket 50–100% (n=5, −9.04%, t=−2.69) is a 5-observation cell — ignore.
  - Surprise >100% (n=38, −4.08%) dominates the book (avg surprise 364% confirms a heavy tail of likely low-quality prints).
- Nothing in these splits licenses a "rescue" of Exp 3. The rule is dead; subgroup mining to resurrect it is exactly what pre-registration exists to prevent.

## Data quality

- Artifact mix is **heavily skewed high-risk**: 52/77 high, 12 medium, 13 low. Average surprise of 364% confirms the feed is dominated by small-base or restated EPS prints rather than clean beats. This is an operational concern for any successor experiment.
- Revenue-leg coverage: 36 yes / 39 no / 2 unknown — roughly half the book had no confirmed revenue beat accompanying the EPS beat. Not an edge claim, just a feed note.
- Annotation completeness: 0 unannotated. Good.

## Candidate pre-registrations

Offered for Exp 4+ consideration only; none to be started until Exp 3's open tail settles.

1. **Exp 4 — Quality-gated PEAD long.** Entry: EPS surprise between +20% and +100% (caps the junk tail) **AND** revenue confirmed Yes **AND** artifact risk ∈ {low, medium}. Size $500, max 20 concurrent, 28-day hold, taker market on Alpaca paper. Metric: excess vs SPY. Kill: n=40 settled, mean excess ≤ 0 kills; t ≥ 2 validates; else extend to n=80. *Motivation:* Exp 3 feed was dominated by high-artifact, uncapped-surprise prints; literature (Bernard-Thomas and successors) consistently finds PEAD concentrated in cleaner surprises.

2. **Exp 5 — PEAD short on high-artifact extreme surprises.** Entry: EPS surprise > +100% **AND** artifact risk = high. Short $500, same concurrency/hold/venue. Kill rule identical structure. *Motivation:* Exp 3's junk-print cohort drifted negatively; worth a pre-registered test rather than post-hoc belief.
