## Observations

- **Headline result (n=60):** mean excess −4.65%, t=−2.26. Directionally opposite to the PEAD literature's long-side drift. Magnitude is economically meaningful, not just statistically negative.
- **Revenue-confirmed split:** yes n=28, mean −3.08%, t=−1.38; no n=30, mean −5.35%, t=−1.53. Both negative; the "cleaner" leg is less bad but still negative. **Preliminary — both sub-cohorts are below the n=40 threshold and this split was not pre-registered. Do not act on it.**
- **Artifact split:** low n=11 (−5.91%), med/high n=49 (−4.37%). Low-artifact cohort is *worse*, cutting against a "noise is diluting a real edge" story. **Preliminary, n<40.**
- **Surprise-magnitude split:** 20–50% n=17, 50–100% n=5, >100% n=38 — all negative. The 50–100% bucket (n=5) is noise. No monotonic pattern suggests tuning the threshold would rescue the strategy. **Preliminary.**
- The full-sample t=−2.26 is the only cohort at or past the pre-registered decision size. Everything below is a forking-path hazard.

## Data quality

- **Artifact mix is heavily high-risk** (52/77 high, 13 low). Average surprise of 364% confirms the universe is dominated by small-base / restructuring-driven EPS prints rather than clean beats. This is an operational note about the EPS-beat universe itself — not a reason to re-slice the result.
- **Revenue confirmation coverage: 36 yes / 39 no / 2 unknown** — roughly half the book lacks a confirmed revenue beat. Relevant for designing Exp 4, not for re-interpreting Exp 3.
- Book of record is complete; no missing fills or annotation gaps.
